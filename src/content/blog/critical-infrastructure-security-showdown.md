I served as a judge for the [Critical Infrastructure Security Showdown (CISS)](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/itrust/ciss-2026/), an international cybersecurity competition focused on operational technology (OT). Teams tackle security challenges using physical critical infrastructure testbeds and digital replicas, putting their skills to the test in environments that reflect industrial systems.
